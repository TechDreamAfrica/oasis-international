1. **Mission-Focused Design**: Every section emphasizes the ministry's work in West Africa
2. **Donation-Centered UX**: Multiple clear calls-to-action for donations
3. **Visual Storytelling**: Uses imagery and statistics to tell the impact story

5. **Accessibility**: Green/white color scheme maintains readability
