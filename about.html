About Us

Home About Us

Our Mission

To spread the Gospel of Jesus Christ throughout West Africa by building churches, supporting orphaned children, and training ministers. We exist to transform lives and communities through the power of God's love, creating lasting spiritual and social impact across the region.

Our Vision

To establish a network of thriving churches across West Africa, where every orphaned child has a loving home, and every minister is equipped with solid biblical training. We envision communities transformed by the Gospel, demonstrating God's love and justice.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Biblical Foundation

We ground our ministry in Scripture, ensuring all church building and training reflects sound biblical principles and doctrine.

Compassionate Care

We demonstrate Christ's love by caring for orphaned children and supporting vulnerable families throughout West Africa.

Church Building

We establish local churches as centers of worship, community, and spiritual growth in every region we serve.

Excellence

We pursue excellence in all aspects of ministry, giving our best for God's glory.

Minister Training

We equip local leaders with solid theological education and practical ministry skills for effective church leadership.

Partnership

We work collaboratively with local communities and international supporters to achieve sustainable ministry impact.

Integrity

We maintain transparency and accountability in all our ministries, ensuring every resource serves its intended purpose effectively.

Our Story

Oasis International Ministries was birthed from a divine calling to reach the unreached in West Africa. What began as a burden for souls has grown into a comprehensive ministry impacting entire communities.

From our headquarters in Poteau, Oklahoma, we coordinate extensive mission work across Ghana, Burkina Faso, and surrounding regions. Our ministry encompasses church planting, orphan care, and minister training, with over 150 churches planted and hundreds of ministers equipped.

Each orphaned child we support and every church we build represents God's heart for the lost and forgotten. We continue to expand this work, trusting God to open doors and provide resources for greater kingdom impact.

What We Believe

Our foundational beliefs

The Trinity

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.

Salvation

We believe salvation is found in Jesus Christ alone, through faith and by God's grace.

The Bible

We believe the Bible is the inspired, infallible Word of God and our ultimate authority.

The Church

We believe in the universal Church as the body of Christ, called to worship, fellowship, and service.

The Great Commission

We believe in fulfilling Christ's command to make disciples of all nations.

Eternal Life

We believe in the resurrection of the dead and eternal life for all who trust in Christ.
